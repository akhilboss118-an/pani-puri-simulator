**Category:** Casual

**Short description (80 chars):**
Run a street pani-puri stall, serve one puri at a time, grow to a world chain.

**Full description:**
Run your own pani puri stall! Crack, fill, dip and serve — one puri at a time.
Build combos, earn tips, chase daily goals and badges, speak Hindi with
customers, and upgrade from a roadside cart to a Pani Puri Palace, Franchise HQ
and World Tour. Fill puris faster with an Automatic Potato Prep Machine. Weather
and festivals, monsoon and Diwali, keep every day fresh. Low-poly 3D, runs on
low-end phones, no ads, offline, free.
